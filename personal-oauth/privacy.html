Privacy Policy

This policy applies to personal OAuth applications operated by the site owner solely for access to the owner's own Google account and services.

Google user data

The application may request access to Google Drive data as required for personal file synchronization and management through rclone.

Use of data

Google user data is used only to perform actions explicitly initiated by the site owner, such as listing, reading, uploading, updating, moving, or deleting files in the owner's own Google Drive.

Storage and sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth credentials and tokens are retained only as needed for the owner's private use and are not intentionally disclosed publicly.

Public availability

The OAuth application is intended for personal, non-commercial use by the site owner and is not offered as a public service.
